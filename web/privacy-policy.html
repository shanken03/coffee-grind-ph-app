Coffee Grind PH — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Coffee Grind PH ("we", "our", or "us") handles information in connection with the Coffee Grind PH Mobile POS application (Package: com.shanken.coffeegrindph).

1. Purpose of the Application

The Coffee Grind PH Mobile POS application is a dedicated internal commercial Point of Sale and inventory tracking tool designed for authorized cafe staff, cashiers, and managers operating at Coffee Grind PH branches (including Santa Cruz, LSPU, and affiliated branches).

2. Information We Collect

To operate branch sales, authenticate staff, and manage cafe inventory, the application collects and processes:

  • Branch Account Authentication: Authorized branch email addresses, user identifiers, and login timestamps to secure access and distinguish branch transactions.
  • Commercial Transaction Records: Order items, drink recipes, order quantities, subtotal amounts, cash tender, exact change, and optional GCash electronic transaction reference codes for bookkeeping and store receipt tracking.
  • Inventory & Serving Quotas: Ingredient stock usage, beverage serving quotas, and restock replenishment requests submitted by branch staff.
  • Device State & Synchronization Logs: Offline transaction queues stored locally on the terminal device until network connectivity is available to synchronize with our secure database.

3. How Information is Used

Information processed by this application is strictly used for:

  • Processing in-store customer sales orders and generating transaction receipts.
  • Deducting physical recipe stock and tracking branch beverage quotas in real-time.
  • Facilitating store restock fulfillment between branch staff and central management.
  • Broadcasting operational announcements and inventory updates to branch terminals.

4. Data Storage and Security

All data is transmitted securely using industry-standard TLS/HTTPS encryption. Backend databases, user authentication, and transaction ledgers are hosted on secure Google Cloud Firebase infrastructure (Cloud Firestore, Firebase Authentication). We do not sell, rent, or trade any transaction, staff, or customer data to third-party advertisers or data brokers.

5. Permissions Required

  • Internet Access: Required to communicate with Cloud Firestore and authenticate authorized branch staff accounts.
  • Network State: Required to intelligently switch between online instant checkout and offline transaction queueing during connectivity outages.

6. Children's Privacy

The Coffee Grind PH Mobile POS application is intended solely for business operations by authorized cafe personnel and staff aged 18 and older. It does not target or collect information from children.

7. Data Retention and Deletion Requests

Commercial transaction records are retained for internal accounting, inventory audit, and tax compliance purposes. Branch accounts and operational logs are managed directly by the store administration. For data inquiries or account deletion requests, please contact store management at malabananshanken03@gmail.com.
