⚡ Offline Order Queue & Auto-Sync:
- Keep selling seamlessly even during internet or network outages.
- Orders queue locally with full data persistence.
- Automatic background synchronisation upon network restoration with overdraft quota safeguards and atomic database batching.

📋 Restock Request Management:
- Staff can submit inventory replenishment requests directly from the branch POS.
- Real-time tracking of pending, approved, and fulfilled restock orders.

📢 Staff Announcements & Notifications:

- Branch-scoped session authorization (Santa Cruz, LSPU).
