7. FIREBASE AUTH & FIRESTORE ALIGNMENT CHECKLIST
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Before distributing to branch staff:
1. Verify SHA-1 & SHA-256 in Firebase Console:
   - In Google Play Console -> Setup -> App integrity -> App signing.
   - Copy the 'SHA-1 certificate fingerprint' and 'SHA-256 certificate fingerprint'
     from the 'App signing key certificate'.
   - Go to Firebase Console (https://console.firebase.google.com/) ->
     Project Settings -> Your apps -> Android app (coffee_grind_ph) ->
     Add fingerprint.
   - Paste both SHA-1 and SHA-256. This ensures Firebase Authentication and
     Google Play App Signing work without rejection.

2. Verify Firestore Security Rules:
   - Ensure the unified ruleset with Section 10 quota and RFID rules remains
     active in coffee-grind-ph-dce9c.

9. OPTIONAL: PACKAGE NAME CHANGE (IF GOOGLE PLAY REJECTS 'com.example')
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
If Google Play Console shows "You must use a different package name because
'com.example' is restricted":
1. Choose your new package name, e.g.: ph.coffeegrind.pos
2. Update android/app/build.gradle.kts:
   - namespace = "ph.coffeegrind.pos"
   - applicationId = "ph.coffeegrind.pos"
3. In Firebase Console, add the new Android App package name 'ph.coffeegrind.pos'.
4. Download the updated 'google-services.json' and place it in 'android/app/'.
5. Run: flutter build appbundle --release
